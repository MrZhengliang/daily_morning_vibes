"Strength is not just in not breaking; it's in rising stronger from the fall."
True strength lies not in never falling but in the power to rise stronger each time we fall. Think of a tree that has endured countless storms; its resilience is not in remaining unscathed but in the tenacity with which it rebuilds and thrives after each gale. Each fall is merely a temporary setback, a momentary pause in the course of our journey. Embrace these moments as opportunities to fortify your resolve and emerge with newfound vigor. Remember, the road to success is paved with the lessons learned from lows, and each rise showcases the true depth of your strength.
